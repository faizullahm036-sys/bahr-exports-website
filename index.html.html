Who We Are

Built on Trust,
Delivered with Care

Bahr Exports is a Kochi-based international trade company dedicated to sourcing and exporting the finest agricultural products from India to markets across the Middle East, Asia, Europe, and beyond.

The name Bahr — meaning "ocean" in Arabic — reflects our mission: to connect continents, bridge cultures, and carry the richness of Indian produce to every corner of the world with reliability and integrity.

With deep-rooted supplier relationships across Kerala and India's agricultural heartlands, we ensure every shipment meets international quality standards and reaches our clients in perfect condition.
